# The first heading becomes the title

This folder, `_drafts`, is never published. To publish a post, put it in
`_posts` with the date at the start of its name:

    _posts/2026-11-23-the-name-in-the-url.md

That file appears at bergmandavid.com/blog/the-name-in-the-url/ about a minute
after you push. The newest date goes on top of the list.

## Writing

Plain paragraphs are separated by a blank line. *Italics*, **bold**,
[links](https://example.com), and `code` work as you'd expect.
